v1.3.75 PROMOTED TRADER POLICY
- Strategy: tp5_nostop_adv30_runner50_trail1_daily_core_persistence_skip_v2
- Admission: Daily-Core + Persistence V2, fail closed
- Risk tiers: STANDARD + HIGH_RISK
- 10 generic slots
- 10% of current MTM equity per new position
- 100% max nominal exposure
- 1x cross
- one open position per symbol
- no stop-loss before TP5
- ordinary trade: close 100% at +5%
- recovery runner: only if the trade first suffered >=30% adverse return before first TP5; realize 50% at TP5, keep 50% in the SAME slot, and trail the remainder 1 return-percentage-point behind the best post-TP5 short return
- runner exchange stop is raised in 0.25 percentage-point steps
- if a MEXC contract cannot be split safely because of min-volume/step rules, fall back to a full TP5 close

Existing open positions are NOT converted to the new exit policy. Each keeps its persisted exit_strategy and frozen metadata.

LIVE EXECUTION IS DELIBERATELY FAIL-CLOSED IN render.yaml
The package ships with:
  TRADING_MODE=paper
  MEXC_LIVE_ORDER_API_ENABLED=false

After deploying/migrating and running preflight successfully, actual live execution requires all of:
  TRADING_MODE=live
  MEXC_LIVE_ORDER_API_ENABLED=true
  MEXC_API_KEY=<Futures-enabled key>
  MEXC_API_SECRET=<secret>
  LIVE_TRADING_CONFIRM=I_UNDERSTAND_LIVE_TRADING

Do not arm live mode until `python -m app.trader_preflight` succeeds and the exchange account has no unmanaged futures positions.
